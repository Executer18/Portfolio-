About Me

A builder with a
practical mindset

I'm a self-taught web developer based in Kolkata, India. I've learned by building, breaking, and rebuilding real projects rather than following a classroom path — and that shows up in how I ship: fast, pragmatic, and focused on what actually matters to a business.

My focus is on frontend craft, backend systems, and infrastructure that costs nothing to run. Every site I build is judged on three things: how fast it loads, how hard it is to break into, and how little it costs to keep alive.

I'm currently taking on freelance projects for businesses anywhere in the world — remote-first, async-friendly, and built around getting you a site that performs like it has a team behind it, without the team-sized bill.
